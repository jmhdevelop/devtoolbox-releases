Privacy

We collect nothing.

Neither DevToolbox nor DevToolbox Lite collects, stores or transmits personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no identifiers and no advertising.

What stays on your Mac

Everything. System metrics, analysis results and your preferences are computed and stored on your own computer. None of it leaves it.

Access to your files

DevToolbox Lite only reads folders you explicitly authorise, plus your Downloads folder. DevToolbox, the full version, reaches the cache and build locations it shows on screen. Neither one sends the contents or the names of your files anywhere.

Network connections

DevToolbox Lite does not connect to the internet. DevToolbox, the paid version, connects only to check whether a new release is available. That check sends no data about you.

Purchases

Payment for the paid version is processed by Stripe, which acts as the seller and handles your billing details under its own policy. We only receive confirmation that the purchase exists, in order to issue the licence.
